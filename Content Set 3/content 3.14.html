Mountains

Mountains are elevated landforms that rise above the surrounding landscape. Many form as Earth's crust is pushed, folded, or broken, while others grow through volcanic activity. Wind, water, and ice reshape their slopes, creating ridges and valleys as rock is gradually worn away.

Key points

  • Different geological processes produce mountains with a variety of visible shapes.
  • Weathering and erosion continue changing mountain landscapes after their initial formation.
