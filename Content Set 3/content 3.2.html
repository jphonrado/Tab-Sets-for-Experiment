Waterfalls

Waterfalls occur where flowing water drops over a steep change in the landscape. The falling water can wear away rock and move loose material downstream. Differences in the strength of rock layers help shape the ledges, pools, and channels around a waterfall.

Key points

  • Waterfall shapes depend partly on the rocks that water flows across.
  • The amount of flowing water can change with rainfall and seasons.
