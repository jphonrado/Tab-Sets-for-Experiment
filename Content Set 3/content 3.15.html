Estuaries

Estuaries are coastal waters where freshwater from land meets saltwater from the sea. Their conditions change with river flow and the movement of tides. Nearby marshes, mudflats, and other habitats support varied communities, while the surrounding land influences the water and sediment entering them.

Key points

  • Estuary water often contains a changing mixture of freshwater and seawater.
  • Rivers and tides influence the habitats found within and around estuaries.
