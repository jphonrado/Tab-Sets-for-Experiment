Volcanoes

Volcanoes are openings where molten rock, gases, and other material can reach Earth's surface. Repeated eruptions may build hills or mountains around the opening. Eruptions differ in character, and the landforms they create include broad slopes, steep cones, craters, and fields of cooled lava.

Key points

  • Molten rock is called lava once it reaches the Earth's surface.
  • Different eruption styles produce a variety of volcanic shapes and deposits.
