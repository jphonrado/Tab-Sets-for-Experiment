Grasslands

Grasslands are landscapes where grasses and other low plants dominate the vegetation. Rainfall, grazing, and fire can influence how these environments develop. Grasslands differ across regions, but their open spaces and extensive plant roots support communities adapted to life with relatively few trees.

Key points

  • Grasslands can differ in plant height, rainfall, soil, and seasonal patterns.
  • Grazing and natural fire help shape many grassland environments over time.
