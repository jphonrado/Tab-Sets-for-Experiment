Glaciers

Glaciers are large bodies of land ice that move slowly under their own weight. They develop where accumulated snow becomes compressed into ice over time. As they move, glaciers carry rock and sediment, reshape valleys, and store water that may later enter rivers through melting.

Key points

  • Glacier ice forms from snow that accumulates and becomes increasingly compressed.
  • Moving glaciers can scrape the ground and transport pieces of rock.
