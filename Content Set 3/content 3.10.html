River Deltas

River deltas are deposits of sediment that build near some river mouths. They develop when a river supplies material faster than waves and currents remove it. Delta channels may divide into branches, and their changing deposits create a mixture of land and shallow water.

Key points

  • A river carries sediment that can accumulate near its meeting place.
  • Waves, tides, and sediment supply influence the shape of a delta.
