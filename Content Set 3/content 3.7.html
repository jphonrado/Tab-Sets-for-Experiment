Caves

Caves are natural openings in rock that extend beneath the ground. Some develop as water slowly dissolves rock, while others form through volcanic activity or erosion. Cave passages can connect to underground water systems, and their darkness creates conditions unlike those at the surface.

Key points

  • Water can gradually enlarge cracks in certain rocks to form passages.
  • Caves vary widely in their size, shape, moisture, and geological origins.
