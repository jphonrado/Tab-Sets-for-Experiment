Rainforests

Rainforests are wooded environments with dense tree cover and many kinds of living things. Their overlapping branches create a canopy that shades the ground below. Different rainforest layers provide different conditions for plants, while fallen leaves return material to the forest floor.

Key points

  • A closed canopy helps create a shaded environment beneath the trees.
  • Rainforest habitats support many plant species within a relatively small area.
