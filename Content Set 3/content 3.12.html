Canyons

Canyons are deep valleys with steep sides, shaped over time by erosion. Rivers are an important force in cutting many canyons, while weathering helps change their walls. Exposed rock layers can reveal parts of the landscape's geological history as erosion removes material above them.

Key points

  • Flowing rivers can gradually cut downward through rock to deepen valleys.
  • Canyon walls often expose layers that formed at different geological times.
