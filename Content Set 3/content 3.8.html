Coral Reefs

Coral reefs are marine structures built largely from the hard skeletons of small animals called coral polyps. Over time, their accumulated skeletons create complex underwater spaces. These spaces support many other organisms, making reefs important habitats rather than simply collections of colorful underwater rocks.

Key points

  • Reef building corals produce hard skeletons from a material called calcium carbonate.
  • The spaces within reefs provide shelter for many different marine organisms.
