Mangroves

Mangroves are trees and shrubs adapted to life along many tropical and subtropical coastlines. They grow in environments affected by salty water and changing tides. Their tangled roots help hold sediment in place and create sheltered spaces where many coastal organisms can live.

Key points

  • Mangrove roots help stabilize coastal sediment in areas affected by tides.
  • These coastal forests provide sheltered habitat around their roots and branches.
