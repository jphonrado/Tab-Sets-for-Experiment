Tidal Pools

Tidal pools are pockets of seawater left among coastal rocks as the tide falls. They become temporarily separated from the wider sea until water returns. Conditions within these pools can change as sunlight, rainfall, waves, and evaporation affect their temperature and salt content.

Key points

  • Water remains trapped in rocky hollows after the surrounding tide retreats.
  • Pool inhabitants experience changing conditions as tides expose and refill habitats.
