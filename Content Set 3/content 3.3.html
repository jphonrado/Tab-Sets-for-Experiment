Wetlands

Wetlands are places where water covers the ground or keeps the soil saturated for part of the year. Marshes and swamps are familiar examples. Their plants and soils provide habitat, slow moving water, and can help trap sediment before it reaches nearby waterways.

Key points

  • Some wetlands remain flooded while others become drier during certain seasons.
  • Wetland plants and soils can reduce the movement of floodwater downstream.
