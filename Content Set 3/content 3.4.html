Sand Dunes

Sand dunes are mounds or ridges built when wind moves and deposits grains of sand. They form where sand is available and conditions allow it to collect. Dunes occur along some coasts and in deserts, and their shapes reflect wind and surrounding landforms.

Key points

  • A dune needs available sand, moving wind, and somewhere to accumulate.
  • Changes in wind direction can gradually alter a dune's overall shape.
