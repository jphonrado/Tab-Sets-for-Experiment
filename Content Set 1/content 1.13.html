Sports

Weightlifting

Olympic weightlifting involves raising a loaded barbell from the floor to an overhead position. Competition uses two lifts called the snatch and the clean and jerk. Athletes attempt progressively selected weights, and their best successful result in each lift contributes to an overall combined total.

Key points

  • The snatch brings the bar overhead in one continuous lifting movement.
  • The clean and jerk first brings the bar to the shoulders.
