Triathlon

Triathlon combines swimming, cycling, and running into one continuous race. Competitors move between the three activities in designated transition areas. The overall result includes the time spent changing equipment, so a smooth transition matters alongside performance during each part. Race distances vary among different triathlon events.

Key points

  • The usual race order is swimming first, then cycling, then running.
  • Athletes manage their effort across all three activities rather than one alone.
