Sports

Gymnastics

Artistic gymnastics combines controlled movement with strength, balance, and coordination. Athletes perform routines on a floor area or on apparatus such as bars, rings, and a beam. Judges consider the difficulty of a routine and how accurately its movements are performed, including control during landings.

Key points

  • An all around competition combines a gymnast's results across several apparatus.
  • Routines link individual skills into a sequence with a controlled finish.
