Table Tennis

Table tennis is played across a table divided by a small net. Players use rackets to send a light ball onto the opponent's half. The ball's speed, direction, and spin affect how it travels and bounces. Matches can involve individual opponents or pairs playing doubles together.

Key points

  • Players must judge both the incoming ball's bounce and its spin.
  • A game normally requires eleven points and a two point winning margin.
