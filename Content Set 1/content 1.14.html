Sports

Wrestling

Wrestling is a combat sport in which two athletes compete on a mat. They use grips, movement, and controlled techniques to gain an advantage over each other. A wrestler can win by pinning an opponent's shoulders or by earning points under the rules of the particular wrestling style.

Key points

  • Freestyle and Greco Roman wrestling differ in the techniques they permit.
  • Referees award points for actions that establish control or expose an opponent.
