Canoe Sprint

Canoe sprint involves racing narrow boats along a straight course on calm water. In canoe events, paddlers kneel and use a paddle with one blade. Races may involve individual paddlers or teammates in the same boat. Competitors try to reach the finish before boats in neighboring lanes.

Key points

  • Racing lanes separate competing boats as they travel along the course.
  • Paddlers coordinate strength and technique to maintain speed throughout the race.
