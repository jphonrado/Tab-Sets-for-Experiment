Badminton

Badminton is played with rackets and a shuttlecock on a court divided by a net. Players aim to land the shuttlecock inside the opposing side's boundaries. Matches may feature one player on each side or pairs working together to cover their side of the court.

Key points

  • A rally continues while players successfully return the shuttlecock across the net.
  • Placement and changes of pace can move an opponent around the court.
