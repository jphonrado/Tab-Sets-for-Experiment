Sports

Archery

Archery involves aiming a bow and releasing arrows toward a target. The target has scoring areas that reward accurate shots. Competitors try to repeat a steady movement while managing their aim and release. Events may involve individual archers or teams shooting in a planned order.

Key points

  • A consistent stance helps an archer repeat the same shooting movement.
  • Arrows closer to the target's center normally receive more scoring points.
