Water Polo

Water polo combines swimming with a team ball game in a pool. Players pass the ball and try to throw it into the other team's goal. Seven players from each side, including a goalkeeper, are in the water during standard play, moving between attack and defense.

Key points

  • Field players generally handle the ball using only one hand at a time.
  • Players remain afloat by swimming or treading water during active play.
