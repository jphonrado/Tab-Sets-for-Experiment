Track Cycling

Track cycling takes place on an oval track called a velodrome. Its events include short sprints, longer races, and team competitions. Riders use speed, timing, and positioning to meet an event's goal. Different events may reward finishing first, recording the fastest time, or collecting points throughout a race.

Key points

  • The track's banked bends help riders negotiate turns at racing speeds.
  • Team events require riders to coordinate their efforts and riding positions.
