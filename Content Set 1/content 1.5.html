Sports

Swimming

Competitive swimming involves completing a course through water as quickly as possible. Pool races use different distances and swimming styles, including backstroke, breaststroke, butterfly, and freestyle. Some races are individual events, while relays divide the race among teammates who enter the water in a set order.

Key points

  • Turns and starts are important parts of a swimmer's overall race.
  • Medley events combine several swimming styles within the same scheduled race.
