Handball

Handball is an indoor team sport centered on throwing a ball into a defended goal. Players pass, dribble, and move into space to build an attack. Seven players from each side, including the goalkeeper, take part on court, while the defending team attempts to prevent clear shooting opportunities.

Key points

  • Passing and movement help attacking players create space near the goal.
  • The goalkeeper protects the net while teammates defend against incoming attacks.
