Sewing Machine

A sewing machine joins pieces of material by making repeated stitches with thread. It became an important tool in clothing production as manufacturers organized work into separate tasks. Workers could cut fabric to patterns and use machines to stitch the prepared pieces into garments.

Key points

  • Machines supported the production of multiple garments based on the same patterns.
  • Factory workers often assembled particular parts instead of making each complete garment.
