History

Bicycle

The bicycle developed through many designs before a familiar arrangement became common. Safety bicycles used wheels of similar size and a chain drive. Improvements in tires and brakes helped make cycling practical for travel and recreation, while bicycle manufacturing also influenced other transport technologies.

Key points

  • Pedals, gears, and a chain helped transfer the rider's effort to a wheel.
  • Historical bicycles show how designers changed wheels, frames, tires, and braking systems.
