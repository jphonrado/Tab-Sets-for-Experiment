Paper Money

Paper money represents value through printed notes issued by an authority such as a bank. Historical notes used designs, names, and other details to identify their issuer. Printing plates and proposed designs show how banknotes were planned and how makers tried to discourage copying.

Key points

  • Decorative details could help distinguish a bank's notes and make copying harder.
  • Surviving designs and printing plates reveal choices made before notes entered circulation.
