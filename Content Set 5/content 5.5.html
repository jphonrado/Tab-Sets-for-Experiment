Typewriter

A typewriter produces printed characters when a person presses keys on a keyboard. Mechanical designs bring a piece of type against paper, usually through an inked ribbon. Developing reliable machines required ways to position letters, move the paper, and prevent moving parts from jamming.

Key points

  • An inked ribbon transfers the shape of each selected character onto paper.
  • Paper moves across the machine so successive letters form a line of text.
