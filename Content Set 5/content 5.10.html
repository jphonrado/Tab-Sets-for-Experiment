Light Bulb

The incandescent light bulb developed through experiments by many researchers and inventors. Its filament produces light when an electrical current heats it. Making useful lamps involved improving the filament, controlling conditions inside the glass bulb, and building systems that could supply electrical power.

Key points

  • Early experiments tested different filament materials to improve useful operating life.
  • A lamp needed supporting equipment such as wiring, switches, and power supplies.
