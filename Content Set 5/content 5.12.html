Compass

A magnetic compass helps a traveler determine direction using a magnetized needle. The needle aligns with Earth's magnetic field, giving a reference for navigation. For centuries, compasses have been important instruments for journeys, particularly when travelers could not rely on visible landmarks alone.

Key points

  • A direction card allows the needle's position to be interpreted by the user.
  • Magnetic north provides a reference that navigators can use alongside other observations.
