Printing Press

A printing press transfers ink from a prepared surface onto paper. In traditional letterpress printing, workers arrange separate pieces of type to form a page. Presses helped printers produce repeated copies of books and other documents, changing how written information was shared.

Key points

  • Movable type lets printers rearrange individual letters for different pages and projects.
  • Mechanical presses gradually changed the work of producing books, newspapers, and leaflets.
