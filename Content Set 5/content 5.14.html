Locomotive

A locomotive provides the power that moves a train along railway tracks. Early steam locomotives combined boilers, cylinders, wheels, and other working parts. Designs such as Rocket brought several improvements together, illustrating how engineers developed machines suited to the expanding railway systems of the nineteenth century.

Key points

  • Steam locomotive development involved improvements to boilers and the movement of exhaust.
  • Preserved railway engines help historians examine changing transport technology and engineering practices.
