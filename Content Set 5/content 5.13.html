Mechanical Clock

A mechanical clock measures time through a controlled arrangement of moving parts. Weights or springs provide energy, while gears carry motion through the mechanism. Over time, clockmakers improved accuracy and developed different forms, making clocks important objects in public buildings and everyday routines.

Key points

  • An escapement releases energy in controlled steps instead of all at once.
  • Surviving clock mechanisms show the skill involved in making gears and regulators.
