Levers

Levers are simple machines consisting of a rigid part that turns around a support called a fulcrum. Applying a force at one position can move a load elsewhere. The distances between the support, effort, and load determine how the lever changes the required force.

Key points

  • A lever's arrangement can trade a longer movement for a smaller force.
  • Seesaws and crowbars are everyday examples of objects using lever principles.
