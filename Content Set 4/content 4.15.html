Satellites

Satellites are objects that move in orbit around another body in space. Some are natural, such as moons, while others are built and launched by people. Artificial satellites carry equipment that can observe Earth, study space, relay communications, or help determine positions on Earth.

Key points

  • An artificial satellite usually needs a power source and communication equipment.
  • Different satellite orbits suit different tasks, including mapping and weather observation.
