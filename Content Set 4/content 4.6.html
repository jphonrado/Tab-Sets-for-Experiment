Telescopes

Telescopes collect light to help observers study distant objects. Optical designs use lenses, mirrors, or combinations of both to form images. Gathering more light can reveal objects that appear faint, while careful focusing helps bring distant features into a clearer view for observation.

Key points

  • A telescope's main lens or mirror gathers light from distant objects.
  • Some telescopes observe from space, above much of Earth's interfering atmosphere.
