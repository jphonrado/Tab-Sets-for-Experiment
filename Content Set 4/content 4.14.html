Optical Lenses

Optical lenses are transparent objects shaped to change the direction of passing light. Their curved surfaces can bring light rays together or spread them apart. Lenses are used in cameras, glasses, and other instruments to form images or change how an object is viewed.

Key points

  • A lens changes a light ray's direction through a process called refraction.
  • Different lens shapes can produce different image sizes and focusing behavior.
