Solar Panels

Solar panels contain cells that convert energy from sunlight into electrical energy. Light absorbed by these cells helps produce an electric current. Panels can be connected into larger groups, supplying energy for devices and buildings when suitable sunlight reaches their exposed surfaces during use.

Key points

  • Photovoltaic cells transform light energy into electricity without burning a fuel.
  • The amount of available sunlight influences how much electricity panels generate.
