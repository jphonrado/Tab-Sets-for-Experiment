Planets

The planets in our solar system travel around the Sun along repeating paths called orbits. They differ in size, composition, atmosphere, and distance from the Sun. Astronomers also study planets orbiting other stars, using observations to learn about the properties of these distant worlds.

Key points

  • Planets orbiting stars beyond our solar system are commonly called exoplanets.
  • Astronomers can detect some distant planets through changes in their stars.
