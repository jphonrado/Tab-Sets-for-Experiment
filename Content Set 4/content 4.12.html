Microscopes

Light microscopes use lenses to reveal details that are difficult to see with unaided eyes. A specimen is placed where light can illuminate it, and a focusing mechanism sharpens the image. Different lens arrangements provide different views and levels of enlargement for careful examination.

Key points

  • A simple light microscope uses a lens to enlarge nearby details.
  • Compound light microscopes combine lenses to produce a more enlarged view.
