Magnets

Magnets produce magnetic fields that can attract certain materials and act on other magnets. A familiar bar magnet has a north pole and a south pole. Depending on how two magnets are positioned, their poles can pull toward each other or push apart.

Key points

  • Opposite magnetic poles attract, while matching poles push away from one another.
  • Materials such as iron can respond strongly to a nearby magnet.
