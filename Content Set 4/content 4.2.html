Gravity

Gravity is the attraction associated with objects that have mass. It keeps objects near Earth's surface and influences the paths of moons and planets. The strength of gravitational attraction depends on mass and distance, so it changes when the objects or their separation change.

Key points

  • A larger mass produces a stronger gravitational attraction at the same distance.
  • Gravity helps guide the orbital motion of planets, moons, and spacecraft.
