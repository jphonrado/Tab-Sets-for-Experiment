Fennec Fox

The fennec fox is a small desert fox native to North Africa. Its large ears help it detect prey and release body heat. Sandy colored fur blends with its surroundings. It usually rests in a burrow during the day and searches for food after temperatures begin to fall.

Key points

  • Fur on the feet provides protection when the fox crosses hot sand.
  • Its varied diet includes insects, small animals, and material from plants.
