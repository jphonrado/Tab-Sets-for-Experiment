Animals

Cheetah

The cheetah is a slender cat known for running quickly during short hunts. Its long legs and flexible body support rapid movement across open ground. A spotted coat helps it blend with vegetation. Rather than sustaining a long chase, a hunting cheetah approaches before making a fast pursuit.

Key points

  • A long tail helps the cheetah balance when changing direction during pursuit.
  • Young cheetahs learn hunting skills while spending time with their mother.
