Animals

Meerkat

The meerkat is a small member of the mongoose family that lives in southern Africa. Groups use underground burrows for shelter and rest. While some animals search for food, another may stand upright to watch the surroundings. Warning calls help the group respond when a predator approaches.

Key points

  • Burrow systems provide shelter from predators and extremes of outdoor temperature.
  • Meerkats use different alarm calls to warn others about approaching dangers.
