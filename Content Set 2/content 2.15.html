Komodo Dragon

The Komodo dragon is a large monitor lizard native to several Indonesian islands. It has strong limbs, a muscular tail, and a forked tongue that helps it investigate scents. Adults feed on other animals and may also eat carcasses. Young dragons spend considerable time in trees for protection.

Key points

  • Komodo dragons use burrows and shade to shelter from changing outdoor temperatures.
  • Their island habitats include wooded areas, hills, and stretches of open grassland.
