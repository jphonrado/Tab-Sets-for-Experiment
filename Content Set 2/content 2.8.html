Giant Panda

The giant panda is a bear with a black and white coat that lives in mountain forests in China. Bamboo forms most of its diet. Strong jaws help it process tough plant material, and its front paws are adapted to holding stems while it strips and chews them.

Key points

  • Giant pandas can climb trees, including when young animals seek safety.
  • Scent marks help pandas exchange information without meeting each other directly.
