Animals

Platypus

The platypus is an Australian mammal that lays eggs and spends time in freshwater habitats. It has a broad bill, webbed feet, and a flattened tail. When searching underwater, it uses the bill to detect prey. It gathers small aquatic animals and returns to the surface to eat.

Key points

  • A platypus rests in a burrow dug into a riverbank or streambank.
  • Its food includes insect larvae, worms, and small animals such as crustaceans.
