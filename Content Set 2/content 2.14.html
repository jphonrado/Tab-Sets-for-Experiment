Snow Leopard

The snow leopard is a mountain cat with thick fur and a long tail. It lives in rocky areas of Central Asia, where its pale patterned coat helps it blend with the landscape. Broad paws assist travel over snow, while a flexible body supports climbing and jumping across steep ground.

Key points

  • The long tail helps the cat maintain balance while crossing rocky slopes.
  • Its prey includes mountain sheep, wild goats, and other available animals.
