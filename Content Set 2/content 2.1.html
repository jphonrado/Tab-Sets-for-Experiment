Capybara

The capybara is a large rodent that lives near water in South America. Its sturdy body, slightly webbed feet, and high placed eyes suit life beside rivers and wetlands. Capybaras eat plants and often live in groups, spending time both on land and in the water.

Key points

  • Eyes and nostrils near the top of the head help it watch while submerged.
  • Group living provides more animals to watch for nearby dangers together.
