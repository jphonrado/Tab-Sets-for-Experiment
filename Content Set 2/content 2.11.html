Animals

Polar Bear

The polar bear is a large bear adapted to Arctic environments. Thick fur and a layer of fat help it retain heat. It uses sea ice as an important platform for hunting seals. Broad paws assist with travel over ice and also help the bear move through water.

Key points

  • Polar bears can swim between areas of ice and nearby coastal land.
  • Mothers use sheltered dens to give birth and protect very young cubs.
