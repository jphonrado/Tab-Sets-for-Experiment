Animals

Hippopotamus

The common hippopotamus is a large African mammal closely associated with rivers and lakes. It spends much of the day in water, which helps protect its skin from drying. At night it commonly leaves the water to graze. Its eyes and nostrils sit high on its broad head.

Key points

  • Hippos can keep much of their body underwater while watching their surroundings.
  • Despite their heavy appearance, hippos can move quickly and defend territory forcefully.
