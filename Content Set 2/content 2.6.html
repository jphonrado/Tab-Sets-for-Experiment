River Otter

River otters are mammals adapted to finding food in rivers, lakes, and other freshwater habitats. Their streamlined bodies, strong tails, and swimming feet help them move underwater. A dense coat helps protect them from cold water, while sensitive whiskers provide information about movement close to the face.

Key points

  • Otters regularly groom their fur to keep its insulating layers working effectively.
  • Their meals often include fish and other small animals found in water.
