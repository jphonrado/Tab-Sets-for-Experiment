Red Panda

The red panda is a small mammal with reddish fur and a long, ringed tail. It inhabits forested mountain areas in Asia and spends much of its time in trees. Bamboo is an important food, while claws and a balancing tail help it move among branches.

Key points

  • Red pandas belong to their own animal family rather than the bear family.
  • Their thick fur and bushy tails help them cope with cool conditions.
