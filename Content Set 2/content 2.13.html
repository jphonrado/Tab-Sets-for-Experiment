Animals

Sea Lion

Sea lions are marine mammals that feed in the ocean and rest on land. They have visible outer ears and strong front flippers used for swimming. Their rear flippers can turn beneath the body, helping them move on shore. Fish and squid are common foods for many sea lions.

Key points

  • Sea lions must return to the surface to breathe air through their nostrils.
  • Groups often gather on beaches or rocks to rest and raise young.
