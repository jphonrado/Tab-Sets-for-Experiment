Animals

Giraffe

The giraffe is an African mammal recognized by its long neck, long legs, and patterned coat. Its height allows it to reach leaves above many other plant eaters. Giraffes use a flexible tongue when feeding, and their distinctive body shape is adapted to moving through open woodland.

Key points

  • Giraffes are the tallest living land animals, with a long reaching neck.
  • Their coats carry patches that vary in shape and pattern among animals.
