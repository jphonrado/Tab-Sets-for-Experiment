Animals

Orangutan

The orangutan is a great ape with reddish hair and long arms. It lives in tropical forests on the islands of Borneo and Sumatra. Grasping hands and feet help it travel among branches. Much of its food comes from trees, and fruit is an important part of its diet.

Key points

  • Orangutans have no tail and use strong limbs to move through trees.
  • Young orangutans spend years with their mothers while learning forest survival skills.
